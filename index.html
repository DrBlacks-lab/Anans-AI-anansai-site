05 / Non-claims

What the public surface does not establish.

The claim ceiling stays narrower than the private architecture ambition.

  • The public proof is not a deployment certificate and does not establish universal agent safety.
  • The released proof does not establish OS-level containment or coverage of every execution path.
  • ANANS is not represented here as a production-ready autonomous company or general AGI.
  • No real capital control, real payment, customer communication, or production deployment is claimed here.
  • Reference to NIST identifies a mapping source; it does not imply NIST validation, acceptance, or endorsement.
  • The public site is not a specification for reconstructing unreleased ANANS implementation or proof-development machinery.
