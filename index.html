07 / Selective autonomy

The target is not “always act” or “always improve.”

The system can distinguish between a novel need, a known need, and no warranted need.

Novel need
Create bounded new work

Development is warranted only when existing capability does not already satisfy the need.

Known need
Reuse retained capability

A repeated need does not force redundant self-modification.

No warranted need
Abstain

Lawful non-action is a first-class outcome.

08 / Non-claims

What the public evidence does not establish.

The public claim is intentionally narrower than the architecture ambition.

  • The public proof is not a deployment certificate and does not establish universal agent safety.
  • ANANS is not a production-ready autonomous company.
  • The Cognitive Exoskeleton has not been independently or externally validated.
  • Generalized long-horizon reliability, unattended production operation, and Founder-attention reduction are not established by the public milestone.
  • No real capital control, real payment, customer communication, production deployment, or other consequential external action is claimed by this page.
  • The system cannot redefine its ultimate purpose or enlarge its own consequential authority.
  • Repeated same-machinery acquisition of distinct new capabilities remains unproven.
  • This is not a claim of general AGI or general autonomous recursive self-improvement.
09 / Next frontier

Pressure the boundary harder before making the system more elaborate.

The public direction is evidence-bearing pressure: harder environments, clearer reviewer artifacts, and narrower claims that survive attempts to break them.

Authority-bound agent assays

Test consequential agent workflows against explicit permitted and prohibited actions without turning a bounded result into a blanket safety claim.

Adverse-environment recovery

Inject failures and environmental changes, then measure recovery quality, stale work, duplication, and authority integrity.

Founder-attention measurement

Measure interventions and semantic corrections rather than inferring reduced Founder burden from one bounded sprint.

Independent review

Prepare reviewer-safe evidence and invite attempts to falsify or break the public claims.
