Governance substrate

Decide before.Bound during. Prove after.

ANANS separates what a machine can do from what it is permitted to do. Authority is established before execution, consequential effects remain bounded during execution, and decisions — including refusals — leave evidence that can be reviewed afterwards.

The governance layer is the substrate beneath autonomous organization.

The public page intentionally describes the constitutional and evidence properties without publishing private runtime internals.

01 / Constitutional law
Capability can increase without authority increasing. Epistemic expansion is not authority expansion.
02 / Principles

Govern the decision boundary, not the model’s confidence.

The governance problem begins before execution. A strong model, a unanimous ensemble, or a successful prior run does not manufacture permission.

Before

Authority already exists

Purpose, scope, resource ceilings, and consequential permissions come from Founder or policy authority rather than from the worker seeking to act.

Core principle
During

Effects remain bounded

The execution boundary admits only work inside the delegated envelope. Ambiguity or missing authority fails closed rather than being interpreted optimistically.

Locally validated
After

Evidence survives the run

Governed decisions and refusals produce reviewable receipts so a later reviewer can distinguish what was requested, allowed, attempted, and withheld.

Public reference exists

REFUSAL IS AN EVIDENCE-BEARING OUTCOME

Purpose is external

The system can reason about how to pursue a purpose, but it does not become the source of that purpose.

Permission is explicit

Consequential action depends on a delegated envelope rather than on inferred intent or model confidence.

Refusal is first-class

Missing, ambiguous, expired, or out-of-scope authority produces a bounded refusal rather than silent continuation.

Evidence is separate from persuasion

Receipts are intended to support later inspection, including adverse evidence and withheld promotion.

04 / Recorded states

A useful governance record distinguishes states that demos often collapse.

ANANS treats requested work, authority, execution, evidence, and promotion as different questions. A local PASS is not a production admission.

Lawful completion
Allowed work completes

The requested effect stayed inside its declared envelope and left a reviewable record.

Fail-closed refusal
No authority, no effect

An unresolved or missing prerequisite produces a refusal without silently widening the worker’s permissions.

Withheld promotion
Local evidence stays local

A component can pass locally while live consequential use, production promotion, or external validation remain withheld.

06 / Public boundary

What is intentionally not published here.

The site should reveal enough to make the claims falsifiable without publishing the private machinery required to reproduce or attack the implementation.

  • High-level constitutional rules and claim boundaries are public.
  • Evidence states, adverse results, and withheld claims are public.
  • The existence of admission, bounded execution, and receipts is public.
  • Exact runtime topology, private policy schemas, internal work-object formats, pressure banks, local paths, unpublished receipts, and operational credentials remain private.
  • Implementation-specific exploit surfaces and bypass-sensitive details are not part of the marketing site.
  • Public research artifacts are curated separately from the private development corpus.
Public proof

A public containment-proof repository exists for a bounded recorded governance run. It is evidence for that recorded run, not a claim that the private production implementation or every execution path has been independently verified.

07 / Non-claims

Governance evidence is not production certification.

The public claim stays bounded to what has actually been demonstrated.

  • ANANS is not represented here as production-ready.
  • No independent party has audited the full architecture, private source, or integrated CE result.
  • A public reference proof does not establish non-bypassability of every private execution path.
  • Local validation does not establish unattended long-horizon reliability.
  • The governance layer does not authorize itself, expand Founder purpose, or grant workers new consequential permissions.
  • The public site is not a specification for reconstructing the private ANANS implementation.
08 / Relation to CE

Governance is what lets organizational autonomy increase without becoming sovereignty.

The Cognitive Exoskeleton is the autonomous organization. ANANS governance is the substrate that keeps purpose and consequential authority outside that organization.
