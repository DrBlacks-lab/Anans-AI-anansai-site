Governance · public overview

Decide before.Bound during. Prove after.

ANANS separates capability from consequential permission. Authority comes from outside the worker seeking to act; ambiguous or missing authority does not become permission; released claims leave evidence that can be inspected afterwards.

Read the principles Inspect the released proof
Public principles only

This page describes constitutional properties, not private implementation topology.

Unreleased development mechanics and proof strategy are intentionally excluded.

01 / Core rule
Ability is not permission. Success is not authority.
02 / Principles

Govern the authority boundary, not the model’s confidence.

A stronger model, a successful prior run, or a compelling rationale cannot manufacture consequential permission.

Before

Authority precedes action

Consequential permission must already exist outside the worker seeking to act.

Constitutional rule
During

Ambiguity fails closed

Missing, expired, or out-of-scope authority produces refusal rather than silent widening.

Bounded principle
After

Evidence survives the decision

Released governance claims include reviewable evidence sufficient to inspect the admitted claim.

04 / Released evidence

Public verification is release-specific.

The current containment-proof repository publishes a bounded released claim and the evidence required to inspect it. A released proof does not turn the public repository into a specification for unreleased ANANS machinery.

Claim

Narrow and versioned

The current release is tied to a specific bounded claim and version.

Verifier

Non-claims

Bound the interpretation

The release explicitly excludes broader conclusions not supported by its evidence.

05 / Disclosure boundary

Public evidence is not public development.

ANANS publishes what is necessary to inspect an admitted public claim. Unreleased implementation details, internal test strategy, development artifacts, credentials, and non-public project material remain private by default.

  • Public proof repositories are evidence membranes, not development workspaces.
  • A future proof is not described publicly before its evidence and disclosure gate are earned.
  • Public release material should minimize unnecessary reconstruction or substitution of unreleased implementation.
  • Nothing on this page grants execution, deployment, spending, or publication authority.
06 / Non-claims

Governance evidence is not production certification.

The public claim remains bounded to the released evidence.

  • ANANS is not represented here as production-ready.
  • The public proof does not establish non-bypassability of every private execution path.
  • Local validation does not establish unattended production reliability.
  • The governance layer does not authorize itself or grant workers new consequential permissions.
